# Diageo (DGE) investment decision log, 9 November 2026

## Decision
Watch retained. More constructive on near-term trading trajectory, not enough evidence for a revised intrinsic valuation or initiation. Existing 1,700p house reference, 1,450p entry threshold, and 2,100p upper review level remain **historical and provisional**. The stored 1,775.5p price is dated 26 October 2026, not a current quote.

## New evidence
- 5 November FY27 Q1 reported sales $4.931bn, +1.2% reported; organic growth +1.4% versus analyst consensus −0.4%.
- Ex-EABL organic sales +0.6%, volume −0.1%, price/mix +0.7%. EABL accounts for +0.8pp group organic sales growth.
- North America −3.6% versus consensus −6.7%, positive relative surprise but ongoing absolute contraction.
- LAC +4.8%, APAC +4.1%, India +3.4%, EMEA +2.4%. LAC volume +7.0% against price/mix −2.2%.
- Chinese white spirits' FY26 weakness (−34.9%) remains unresolved, Q1 regional APAC growth does not isolate China.
- FY27 guidance **unchanged**: broadly flat organic sales; low-to-mid-single-digit organic operating-profit growth; FCF ~$2bn after ~$850m exceptional restructuring cash costs; capex ~$1.25bn; leverage midpoint of 2.5–3.0× contingent on disposals.

## Interpretation
A lower bar was beaten. US shipment timing and continued tequila weakness prevent the read-through that the category has recovered. Broad regional growth supports the portfolio's resilience, but cash profitability and quality of price/mix remain incomplete. No numerical DCF update is warranted from a single Q1 sales release.

## Decision triggers
US market share/depletions, tequila/Crown Royal brand trends, Chinese white spirits sales and inventory, sustained LAC margin quality, ex-EABL growth, post-restructuring owner cash flows, and demonstrated deleveraging.
