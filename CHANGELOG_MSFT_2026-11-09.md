# Microsoft (MSFT) investment decision log, 9 November 2026

## Decision
**Watch for new capital; retain an appropriately sized existing holding.** Stronger Azure demand and infrastructure utilisation evidence does not yet offset uncertainty over replacement-adjusted returns on fast-depreciating AI hardware. No transaction recommended.

## New evidence (28 October FY27 Q1)
- Revenue $92.3bn (+19%), operating income $45.9bn (+21%), non-GAAP EPS $4.89 (+18%) excluding OpenAI investment effects.
- Azure $33.2bn (+48%); Microsoft Cloud $63.9bn (+30%); M365 commercial cloud +17% reported, +18% adjusted for prior-year recognition.
- Agents and Infra $76.8bn revenue, $40.6bn operating profit under new FY27 segments. Historical FY26 segments are not directly comparable.
- Commercial RPO $677bn (+73%), ~30% scheduled to recognise within 12 months; commercial bookings −12%, affected by prior large Azure deals.
- Operating cash flow $58bn; cash property and equipment spend $39.4bn; resulting free cash flow $18.6bn, versus ~$25.7bn prior year.
- Total capex $45.2bn including leases, ~$7.4bn finance leases; approximately three-quarters short-lived infrastructure.
- FY27 Q2 Azure 49–50% constant-currency growth outlook, Q2 capex >$50bn; calendar-2026 capex ~$170bn. Management expects FY27 FCF positive.

## Interpretation
Positive: Cloud growth and underlying utilisation, Foundry revenue more than tripled, Copilot adoption and higher premium-product ARPU.
Negative/uncertain: AI equipment replacement risk, step-up in capital expenditure, weaker traditional SKU execution, bookings concentration, and material divergence between adjusted earnings and available free cash flow.
Historical August valuation scenarios ($420/$540/$640; action references $510/$650) are **retained as historical** in the report but withdrawn as current numerical index thresholds pending a new owner-earnings underwriting. The dated $524.51 reference is 23 October, not a live quote.
