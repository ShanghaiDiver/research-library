01

The business

Alphabet is Google: the dominant search engine and its advertising machine, YouTube, the Android and Chrome platforms, Google Cloud, and the DeepMind/Gemini AI research effort, with Waymo and other bets on the side. Search remains one of the best businesses ever built, a near-monopoly on intent with structural margins. What is new is that Cloud has crossed from a cash-burning also-ran into a large, fast-growing, genuinely profitable third pillar, which changes the shape of the whole company.

02

The quarter that argues with the bears

Q2 2026: consolidated revenue $119.8bn, up 24% and the twelfth straight quarter of double-digit growth; operating income up 30% to $40.8bn at a 34% margin. Search & other grew 17% to $63.3bn, YouTube ads 13%, and Google Cloud surged 82% to $24.8bn with operating income more than tripling to $8.8bn (a 35.6% margin). The headline $9.11 EPS is not real, it includes ~$99bn of unrealised gains on equity stakes, so ignore it and value the operations. The market's reaction was telling: the stock fell after hours, not on the results but on capex, guidance was lifted to $195-205bn, then recovered as the cloud-acceleration story sank in.

That search trajectory, from 10% growth up to 19% and holding at 17% even as AI Overviews and Gemini roll out, is the single most important fact in the file: the disruption the multiple fears is not visible in the revenue.

03

04

The case, both ways, and the variant

Bull. A dominant, cash-gushing franchise with a second engine (Cloud) inflecting to serious profit, world-class AI (Gemini, DeepMind, custom TPUs), at 24 times, growing 24%, with the market pricing a disruption the revenue does not show.

Bear. Search is the profit engine and it is the thing most exposed if users shift to conversational AI, the capex is enormous and rising, an antitrust overhang lingers, and if search growth ever does roll over, the whole valuation resets.

Variant perception

The consensus fear is that generative AI disintermediates Google Search, the way it is feared to disintermediate RELX, and the discount to peers is the price of that fear. The variant view, and the one the numbers support, is that Google is an AI beneficiary: it owns the query relationship, the ad system, the distribution (Chrome, Android), the models and the custom silicon, and is folding AI into Search (AI Mode, Overviews) in ways that so far grow revenue rather than cannibalise it, while Cloud rides the same AI wave to 82% growth and real margins. The bear's fair point is that this is the one megacap risk that is genuinely structural to the core profit pool, so it cannot be waved away, only monitored. But the framework's rule is not to pay the fear when the evidence contradicts it, and at 17% search growth the evidence contradicts it. That makes Alphabet a Buy, with the search-growth line the exact thing to watch quarter by quarter.

05

Currency and fit

Dollar earnings, dollar quote, and you already hold the Class C line, so like Microsoft this deepens the US tilt rather than diversifying it, and Alphabet also sits inside your index trackers. The Buy is about quality and value; the sizing question is about not stacking more America on ~70%. Add on weakness, in a size that respects that.

06

What would change the call

  • Downgrade if Search & other growth decelerates materially for two or more quarters (the AI-disruption thesis turning real), if Cloud growth stalls, or if capex keeps rising with no margin payoff.
  • Conviction rises if Search holds double-digit growth through the AI transition and Cloud sustains 30%-plus at expanding margins, which would justify a re-rating toward peers.
07

Verdict

A dominant franchise on a discounted multiple for a disruption the numbers keep contradicting, with a second engine inflecting to real profit. Track A. Fair value ~$400 against ~$345, base return ~12%. Action: Buy / add, sized for the US concentration.

Alphabet is RELX writ large: the cheapest megacap, marked down for an AI-disruption fear, while the very number that fear predicts should fall, Search revenue, keeps growing at 17%, and a second profit engine roars at 82%. The framework does not pay a discount for a fear the evidence contradicts, so it is a Buy. The honesty is in the caveat: this is the one megacap whose central risk really is structural to its core, so unlike Microsoft it must be watched at the level of the search-growth line, not just admired. And, as with Microsoft, the practical constraint is concentration, you hold it, the trackers hold it, so the right expression is adding on weakness in a measured size rather than a large fresh initiation on top of an already-American book.
